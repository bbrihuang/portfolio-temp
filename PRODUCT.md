# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: internship recruiters and hiring managers skimming to decide whether to interview Brian. Also: engineers and club peers who judge by depth of project write-ups, admissions and scholarship reviewers, and potential small-business clients of GradientV or Cubic Builds.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Brian Huang, a Computer Engineering student at Boston University (B.S., expected May 2029). It presents his experience, projects, clubs, and desk setup, and gets visitors to a resume, email, GitHub, or LinkedIn. Success is an interview or inquiry.

## Positioning

Range across software and hardware in one person: local LLMs and robotics, sensors and PCB-level builds, rocket nozzle design, plus real businesses (GradientV software for small businesses, Cubic Builds custom PCs).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack, to be kept: React 19, Vite, Tailwind 4, React Router 7, deployed on Vercel. Content lives in `src/data/*` and `src/config.js`.
- Pages: Home (hero, profile card, clubs, About), Experience, Projects and project detail (case studies, galleries), Setup, 404.
- Site is declared dark (`color-scheme: dark`).
- Currently signals "Open to internships". Locations: Boston, MA / New York City, NY.

## Brand Commitments

Name and voice are Brian's own, first-person and plain. The hero line "I build where software meets hardware" is the current statement of positioning.

## Evidence on Hand

Real content in `src/data/experience.js`, `projects.js`, and `skills.js`; resume PDF at `public/Brian_Huang_Resume.pdf`; build photos for the Room Temperature Monitor; club and company logos in `public/`. Do not fabricate metrics, testimonials, clients, or projects beyond what these files contain.

## Product Principles

1. Show range as one coherent person, not a list of unrelated interests.
2. Let real work (projects, write-ups, photos) carry the proof; never invent claims.
3. Serve a fast skim for recruiters and depth for engineers.
4. Make contact and resume one step away from anywhere.
